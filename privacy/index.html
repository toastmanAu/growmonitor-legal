Grow Monitor privacy policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026
App: Grow Monitor (Android, com.growmonitor)
Contact: toastmanau@gmail.com

The short version

Grow Monitor keeps your grow records on your phone. The developer does not run a server for the app, has no accounts, and does not receive your records, photos or measurements.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage: grow spaces, runs and plants, strain names you type, journal entries and measurements, light readings and profiles, reminders, settings and your photos.

The developer cannot see this data. It is not sent to the developer or to any analytics or advertising service, because the app has none.

Camera and photos

  • The app asks for camera permission only when you use camera light-reading mode or "Take photo".
  • Camera frames used for a light reading are processed on your phone to calculate a reading. They are not stored or sent.
  • Photos you add or take are saved in the app's private storage on your phone and nowhere else. While you take a photo, the camera app briefly writes it to the app's temporary cache folder on your phone, and old temporary files are cleaned up automatically. If you remove an original photo that you had kept, it is moved to a hidden folder in the app's private storage and erased after 30 days. All of this stays on your phone.
  • By default the app saves a copy of an imported photo without its location data. If you choose "Keep original", the app warns you that the location data stays in the file and that anyone you share it with could see it.
  • The app does not use your phone's location.

Google Play

  • Grow Monitor Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. When you view the price, buy, restore or when the app verifies that you own Pro, the app contacts Google Play. Google, not the developer, handles your payment and purchase information under Google's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Google's billing library may also send diagnostics to Google.
  • The app records on your phone that you own Pro so it can work offline. This record stays on your phone.
  • Because there is no developer server, ownership checks happen on your phone with Google Play.

Network use

The app's own code makes no network connections. Its only network contact is with Google Play, described above. The app has network permissions because Google's billing library requires them. There are no ads, analytics or tracking in the app.

Android backup and device transfer

Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for the app's data. To move your records to another phone, use the app's own backup (below). Because of this, a new or restored phone starts without your records until you restore a backup file.

Backups and exports you create

  • Backup: you can save your data, including photos, to one file protected with a password you choose, in a location you choose. Only you hold the file and the password; the app does not keep or send the password. If you lose the password, the file cannot be opened.
  • Not in a backup: your settings (units, theme, currency and tariff) are not saved in the backup file. After restoring on another phone, set them again.
  • Reports: you can export a run as a PDF or CSV file, in a location you choose. These files are not encrypted and contain your grow records, and the PDF can include photos. The app warns you before saving one. Keep them somewhere private.
  • When you save a backup or report to a cloud-synced folder or share it, the service or person you choose receives it. That is outside the app's control.

Deleting your data

The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on a server. On your phone, you can delete the app's data by uninstalling the app or by using Android's "Clear storage" for Grow Monitor. Backup files and exported reports are files you saved yourself; delete them where you saved them. The app does not currently offer an in-app erase-all.

Children

Grow Monitor is not directed to children and is intended for adults.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, for example by adding notifications or an online feature, this policy will be updated before the change ships and the effective date above will change.
